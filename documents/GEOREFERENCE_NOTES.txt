Why calibration is separated from the picture:
The Discovery map is an isometric illustration, not a north-up GIS/survey raster. A simple
latitude/longitude bounding box is therefore not enough. The project uses a projective
GPS <-> pixel transform seeded by named control points.

For the final high-accuracy version:
1. Stand at 6-12 landmarks distributed around the campus.
2. Record GPS latitude/longitude and accuracy for each landmark.
3. Identify the exact same landmark point on the 2D map.
4. Replace/add the anchors in js/tdtu-georef.js and refit the matrix.
5. Later add altitude/floor calibration separately.

Do not claim indoor/survey-grade accuracy until the field-surveyed anchors are added.
