TDTU routing/search update

- Search suggestions now use a curated TDTU campus catalogue first.
- Official labels are shown on the map from zoom 18+.
- Map bounds include the wider campus/VFIS area.
- Selected start/destination is snapped immediately to the nearest walkable approach.
- Routing graph rejects paths through buildings/water and fence/wall barriers.
- A* prefers pedestrian/footway/service corridors and penalizes minor paths/steps.
- No straight snap connector is drawn through grass/buildings.

The catalogue can be refined later with field-surveyed entrance coordinates without changing the UI/router.
